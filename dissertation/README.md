## What it covers

The dissertation pairs a corpus of engagement-pod posts with a topic-by-topic
reading of the rules in force. The corpus holds 397,192 records from four
sources, which reduce to 273,771 distinct posts. Each of the twenty largest
topics gets its own chapter, which asks which laws a regulator, a competitor
or a court would examine first, which actor each law reaches (the author, the
pod-tool operator, the engaging member or the platform), what the rules mean
for readers and for young people, and which questions no court or agency has
answered. Chapter 2 sets out the data, the method, the evidence tiers and
the limits.

## Privacy

The dissertation follows the same rules as the rest of this repository. Its
analysis is aggregate-only: no chapter identifies an account, an author, a
company or an individual post, and no quotation is long enough to identify
its source (Section 2.5). It makes no finding that any author violated any
law. No raw data is included here.

## How it relates to this repository

Section 2.1 says what this repository holds and what it does not. The
repository analyzes the podawaa, HyperClapper and LinkBoost-2025 files and
the 65 export files in aggregate. It does not hold the de-duplication rule
(Section 2.2), the topic model (Section 2.3) or the counts that depend on
them, including the 273,771 distinct posts and the topic shares, so a figure
from the repository and a figure from the dissertation for the same source
can differ. The dissertation cites the repository as it stood at commit
2c8d3f8 on 22 October 2026.
