# Researcher counts 52,232 engagement-pod posts and finds "honest" in about seven times as many as the words for lying

Press release by Dream IT Software. Published on Cyber Defense Wire on 5 November 2026:
<https://cyberdefensewire.com/researcher-counts-52232-engagement-pod-posts-and-finds-honest-in-about-seven-times-as-many-as-the-words-for-lying/>

The text below is the release as published, with one unfilled placeholder removed: the first citation's link to the article, which was never filled in. The Cyber Defense Wire page is the published version.

---

*Mentions of "honest" are rising, mentions of kindness are falling, and no post says "I lied"*

**TL;DR:** Daniel Hall counted kindness, honesty and lying in 52,232 posts from one engagement-pod tool. "Honest" or "honesty" appears in 25 of every 1,000 posts and the words for lying in 4, so "honest" shows up in about seven times as many posts. Use of "honest" has grown from about 1 in 90 posts in late 2024 to about 1 in 35 in 2026, kindness posts are about half as common as they were in 2025, and no post says "I lied." The count measures words and does not test whether any post is true.

Daniel Hall, CEO of Dream IT Software and an author who has worked with engagement pods since 2019, has published an analysis of how often 52,232 posts from one engagement-pod tool mention kindness, honesty and lying. Every post was submitted to the tool to get likes and comments.

An engagement pod is a group of people who agree to like and comment on each other's posts, so that the posts look popular. Some tools run the pods for their members.

Out of every 1,000 posts, 25 use the word "honest" or "honesty," 18 mention kindness and 4 talk about lies. "Honestly" appears in 785 posts. Among the posts that use "honest" or "honesty," about 1 in 4 (322 posts) also use a phrase such as "to be honest" or "let's be honest."

Other figures from the article:

- **"Honest" is growing.** About 1 in 90 posts created in late 2024 used "honest" or "honesty," and about 1 in 35 posts created in 2026 did, which is more than two and a half times as often. About 1 in 3 of the "honest" posts also contain words about selling, marketing or prices.
- **Kindness is declining.** About 2 in 100 posts mentioned kindness in 2025, and a little over 1 in 100 did in the second half of 2026. Of every 100 kindness posts, 58 also use words about leaders or workplaces and 12 mention children, teenagers or students. Words such as "comment below," "follow me" and "repost" appear in 15 of every 100 kindness posts and in 7 of every 100 posts overall.
- **Few posts address lying.** Only 191 posts use "lie" in the sense of lying. The most common forms are "the biggest lie about..." (66 posts), someone is lying to you (30), "the data doesn't lie" (25) and you are lying to yourself (17). No post says "I lied," "I have lied," "I was lying" or "we lied." Posts about scams, fakes, fraud or misleading things number 1,118.

Hall's advice to readers is short. "My rule: when a post tells you it is honest, ask what the post wants from you," he writes. He also writes: "Being kind costs nothing. A post about being kind often asks for something anyway."

## Method and limits

The posts come from 65 export files from one engagement-pod tool. Hall removed hashtags and web links, matched whole words and read a sample of the matches by hand. A few matches were wrong, and the kindness count is a little high because some posts matched a wider word such as "generous." He reports that the gap between "honest" and "lie" is too large for those errors to change it.

The analysis counts words and does not check whether any claim in any post is true. It names no writer and does not say that any writer broke a rule or a law. Of the 65 files, 22 hold exactly 5,000 records, which looks like an export limit, so the figures describe the posts in these files and not every post the tool handled. The raw files are not published, because each record carries the name of its poster.
