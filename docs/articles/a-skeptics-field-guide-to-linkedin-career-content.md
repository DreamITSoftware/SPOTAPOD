# A Skeptic's Field Guide to LinkedIn Career Content

### Five checks, built from a year of verified findings, for anyone who wants to read LinkedIn career content more skeptically

This closes the audience-specific series with a practical synthesis: the actual checks this project's own findings suggest are worth running before taking a piece of LinkedIn career content at face value.

## Check one: does the engagement number match the content's actual reach claims?

A documented, active engagement-pod industry exists specifically to inflate likes, views, and comments. A high engagement count is weak evidence of genuine reach or resonance on its own.

## Check two: is the text identical or near-identical to other posts you've seen?

This project verified 533 identical copies of one resume-rewrite template alone, among several other documented duplicate-content clusters. Template propagation through a network is common and doesn't require any individual post to be original or independently earned.

## Check three: does the account's posting pattern look sustainable for one person?

This project documented accounts posting daily for over 700 consecutive days without a gap, a velocity more consistent with managed or automated operations than individual activity, though not proof of either in any specific case.

## Check four: is the number being cited actually rechecked, or just repeated?

This project's own biggest correction (1,706 to 533 on a resume-template count) came from independently recomputing a number that had apparently only ever been cited, not rechecked. The same applies to any statistic in career content. Repetition isn't verification.

## Check five: is the claim a "signal" or a "finding"?

A keyword rate, an anomaly detection, or a pattern match describes something detected by a specific method with specific limitations. It doesn't, on its own, prove a broader conclusion. Distinguishing "we found a pattern" from "this proves X" is worth doing for any statistic-backed claim, including this project's own.
