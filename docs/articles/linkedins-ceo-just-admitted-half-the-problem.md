# LinkedIn's CEO Just Admitted Half the Problem [Template]

### A format ready to fill in the moment LinkedIn leadership next comments publicly on engagement authenticity

This is a framework piece, not a report on an event that has occurred. It sets out the recurring format this project would use to respond whenever LinkedIn's leadership next makes a public statement on engagement authenticity, following the pattern used for the earlier Shapero/WSJ commentary.

## The structure this format follows

**What was said.** Quote the statement directly, without paraphrase, and cite the primary source: an earnings call transcript, an interview, a company blog post.

**What this project's data can and can't confirm.** Check any specific claim in the statement (a stated detection rate, a stated policy change, a stated scale of the problem) against this project's own independently verified figures where they overlap, and say where they don't overlap.

**What "admitting half the problem" would mean.** This project's own findings span at least two structurally distinct manipulation models (like-based and comment-based) across three separate tools. A statement addressing only one of those, or addressing detection without addressing account-concentration patterns, or vice versa, would be partial, and this format exists to say so without over-crediting or dismissing an incomplete acknowledgment.

## Why this format is prepared in advance

Responding accurately to a fast-moving news event requires deciding beforehand what standard of evidence a future claim will be held to. With the structure prepared, a future response can focus on checking the statement against the data rather than deciding methodology under time pressure.

## What this is, and isn't

**A prepared format, not a report on a specific event.** No claim is made here that LinkedIn's leadership has made any such statement as of this writing.
