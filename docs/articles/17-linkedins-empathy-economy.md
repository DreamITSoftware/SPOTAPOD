Empathy-related language (empathy, empathetic, empathize) shows up in 1,188 records (0.556%) of a 213,491-post dataset, 431 records (0.873%) of a 49,369-post dataset, and 1,473 records (1.889%) of a 77,969-record dataset. LinkBoost-2025's rate is more than 3x podawaa2024's.

## The part worth noticing: this template found itself twice

While checking a completely unrelated keyword search, for burnout and career-transition language, this project's scripts independently surfaced a post titled something like "Empathy isn't just a checkbox for leaders," repeated 56 times in LinkBoost-2025. The empathy-specific scan found the exact same post again, from a completely different search angle.

That's not a coincidence worth shrugging off. When two independent keyword searches, built for different purposes, both stumble onto the same repeated template, it's much stronger confirmation that the template is a genuinely recurring piece of content, not an artifact of one search's particular phrasing.

No single string dominates enough to suggest the whole category is one templated artifact. This is genuine, if repetitive, signal.
