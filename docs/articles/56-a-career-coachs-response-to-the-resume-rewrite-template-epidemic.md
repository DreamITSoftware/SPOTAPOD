# A Career Coach's Response to the Resume-Rewrite Template Epidemic

### Verified: 533 posts running the exact same resume-rewrite formula. Here's what that means for coaches whose clients keep asking about it.

Career coaches field a specific, recurring client question: "I saw this LinkedIn post with a resume trick that got 10,000 likes. Should I be doing that?" This project's data gives a concrete answer to why that question deserves more skepticism than it usually gets.

## The finding behind the question

533 posts in this project's dataset use an identical resume-rewrite template, the same structural formula, reproduced verbatim across hundreds of separate accounts. That's not 533 independent professionals each discovering the same effective technique. It's one piece of content propagating through a network, very possibly with engagement-pod amplification behind at least some of its reach, given the broader patterns this project documents in the same datasets.

## The reframe worth offering clients

The relevant question isn't "does this formula work," because "533 identical copies exist" isn't actually evidence either way about effectiveness. Template propagation and effectiveness are separate questions this data can't jointly answer. The more useful reframe is: a resume format's popularity on LinkedIn is not itself evidence of its effectiveness for an actual hiring decision, and clients chasing the most-liked version of a template are optimizing for the wrong signal. What worked structurally for a hiring manager reading an actual resume is a different question than what generated LinkedIn engagement.

## Why this is worth incorporating into coaching practice

Clients often arrive anxious about a specific viral format they feel they're "supposed" to be using. Being able to point to a concrete, verified number, 533 identical copies of one template, gives a coach a specific, credible reason to redirect that anxiety toward substance rather than format-chasing, instead of relying on a vaguer "don't just copy what you see online" intuition.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**: 533 identical resume-rewrite-template posts, independently reproducible.

**INFERENCE**: that engagement-pod amplification specifically drove any given instance's reach. This project does not claim to know the reach mechanism for any specific post.
