# LinkedIn's Empathy Economy

### The same "empathy isn't just a checkbox" post shows up in two completely different keyword searches

Empathy-related language (empathy, empathetic, empathize) shows up in 1,188 records (0.556%) of a 213,491-post dataset, 431 records (0.873%) of a 49,369-post dataset, and 1,473 records (1.889%) of a 77,969-record dataset. LinkBoost-2025's rate is more than 3x podawaa2024's.

## One template found twice

While checking an unrelated keyword search, for burnout and career-transition language, this project's scripts independently surfaced a post titled something like "Empathy isn't just a checkbox for leaders," repeated 56 times in LinkBoost-2025. The empathy-specific scan found the exact same post again, from a different search.

When two independent keyword searches built for different purposes both find the same repeated template, that is stronger confirmation that the template is recurring content and not an artifact of one search's phrasing.

## The rest of the content

Checked manually, LinkBoost-2025's top repeated empathy-related strings span distinct leadership and emotional-intelligence posts: a listening-skills post, an "understand a person" post, a brand-with-heart post, and, separately, a batch of ChatGPT email-writing prompts that happen to use the word "empathetic." No single string dominates enough to suggest the whole category is one template.
