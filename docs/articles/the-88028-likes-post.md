# The 88,028-Likes Post That Nobody Saw

### One record in a 213,491-post dataset shows more likes than most viral posts get views, and zero views of its own

Somewhere in a 2024 export of LinkedIn posts sits a single record with **88,028 likes** and **0 views**.

Not low views. Not an undercount. Zero, the same number LinkedIn assigns to a post that hasn't loaded on anyone's screen yet.

## Why a single post is worth an entire article

It would be easy to treat this as a rounding artifact if it were the only one. It isn't: 75,442 other posts in the same dataset show the same zero-views-with-likes pattern, at scale. But this one is worth isolating because it's the most extreme single example, the highest like count attached to a zero view count anywhere in the file.

For comparison, a view count of zero should mean nobody's LinkedIn feed algorithm even served this post to a single scroll. Eighty-eight thousand people apparently disagreed enough to click "like" on something that, per LinkedIn's own tracking, none of them ever loaded.

## What this looks like next to real numbers

Most genuinely viral LinkedIn posts, the kind that get picked up by aggregator accounts, screenshotted into newsletters, and argued about in comment sections, report view counts in the millions before their like counts crack five figures. A ratio of thousands of likes to zero views doesn't just break that pattern. It has no pattern to belong to at all.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**, directly reproducible from the source file: this exact record (88,028 recorded likes, 0 recorded views) exists in the 213,491-post dataset described in this project's documentation.

**Not established here**: what specific mechanism produced this number, or whether the post itself was ever posted by a real, willing participant versus generated or boosted through a third-party tool. This project profiles the dataset's structure. It does not make claims about any single account's intent.

Full dataset documentation and every other anomaly of this kind: **github.com/DreamITSoftware/SPOTAPOD**.
