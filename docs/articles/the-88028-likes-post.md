# The 88,028-Likes Post That Nobody Saw

### One record in a 213,491-post dataset shows more likes than most viral posts get views, and zero views of its own

Somewhere in a 2024 export of LinkedIn posts sits a single record with **88,028 likes** and **0 views**.

The view count is zero, the same number LinkedIn assigns to a post that hasn't loaded on anyone's screen yet.

## Why this post

This could be dismissed as a rounding artifact if it were the only one. It is not: 75,442 other posts in the same dataset show the same zero-views-with-likes pattern. This one is the most extreme example, the highest like count attached to a zero view count anywhere in the file.

A view count of zero should mean LinkedIn's feed never served the post to anyone. Yet 88,028 likes were recorded on a post that, per LinkedIn's own tracking, nobody loaded.

## What this looks like next to real numbers

Most viral LinkedIn posts, the kind picked up by aggregator accounts and screenshotted into newsletters, report view counts in the millions before their like counts reach five figures. A ratio of thousands of likes to zero views fits no such pattern.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**, directly reproducible from the source file: this exact record (88,028 recorded likes, 0 recorded views) exists in the 213,491-post dataset described in this project's documentation.

**Not established here**: what specific mechanism produced this number, or whether the post itself was ever posted by a real, willing participant versus generated or boosted through a third-party tool. This project profiles the dataset's structure. It does not make claims about any single account's intent.

Full dataset documentation and every other anomaly of this kind: **github.com/DreamITSoftware/SPOTAPOD**.
