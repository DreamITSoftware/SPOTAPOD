# LinkedIn vs. Being Human

### "Humanity" and "human connection" language is 5x more common in LinkedIn's newest dataset than its oldest

As AI-generated content climbs across LinkedIn, so does a very specific counter-genre: posts explicitly about "being human," "human connection," "human touch," and "humanity" itself. That language appears in 857 records (0.4014%) of a 213,491-post dataset, 374 records (0.7576%) of a 49,369-post dataset, and 1,614 records (2.0701%) of a 77,969-record dataset. That's roughly a 5x climb from the lowest to the highest rate.

| Term | podawaa2024 | HyperClapper | LinkBoost-2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Humanity | 478 | 238 | 711 |
| Human connection | 136 | 62 | 319 |
| Human touch | 154 | 27 | 207 |
| Human side | 29 | 19 | 239 |
| Being human | 27 | 33 | 228 |

## Why "human" alone was deliberately left out

A bare search for the word "human" was ruled out from the start. It collides too broadly with "human resources," "human capital," and generic AI-versus-human comparisons to reliably capture the specific "being human" theme this scan targets. Only the more specific phrases above were counted, a narrower net that trades some recall for a real signal instead of noise.

## What the actual posts look like

Checked manually against LinkBoost-2025's top repeated matches (no single string dominates, so no correction was needed): a light-and-dark duality post about humanity and love, a "spend your life truly living" post, "employee loyalty is dead" commentary, and a post about letting go in relationships. Motivational and leadership-framed, not philosophical or academic.
