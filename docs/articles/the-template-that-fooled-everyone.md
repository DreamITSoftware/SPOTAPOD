# The Template That Fooled Everyone

### One exact sentence appears 533 times across LinkedIn's career-advice content

"No replies. No interviews. Just silence for 90 days. Then, I ran my resume through ChatGPT. Got 5 callbacks in less than a week, using just 8 prompts."

That exact sentence (word for word, punctuation and all) appears **533 times** across a combined 340,829 LinkedIn posts pulled from three different sources.

## Why one template matters more than many vague mentions

Thousands of posts mention resumes, ChatGPT, or job rejection separately, but those are common words, and counting them tells you little. Here the same narrative, numbers, and structure are repeated 533 times by what are presented as 533 different professionals sharing a personal story.

A rejection-to-success narrative like this one is credible to a job seeker because it reads as personal testimony. Five hundred and thirty-three "personal" testimonies sharing one script undercut that credibility.

## What this looked like before it was checked

A third-party document claimed this specific template appeared 1,706 times. That number does not reproduce against the raw source files. The actual, exact-match count is 533, still a large number, but not the one originally claimed. The 1,706 figure appears to have come from a looser matching method that also picked up related-but-different variants of the story, rather than exact repeats of the same template.
