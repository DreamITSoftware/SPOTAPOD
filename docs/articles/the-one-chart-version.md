# The One-Chart Version

### The zero-view finding as a single visualization

Most of this project's articles carry a paragraph of methodology before the number lands. This piece does the opposite: one chart, minimal text, the finding first.

## The chart

A single bar comparison: of 213,491 posts in podawaa2024, **75,442 (35.3%)** show zero recorded views despite carrying recorded likes. Plotted as two bars, "posts with recorded views" and "posts with zero recorded views despite likes," the scale of the anomaly is visible without any explanation.

## Why this format is worth testing at all

Text-first research writing assumes the reader will follow an argument in order: setup, method, number, implication. A chart-first format assumes that showing the number's scale immediately, before any caveats, makes a stronger first impression, with the text underneath carrying the caveats.

## The trade-off

A chart alone can't carry an evidence tier, a methodology note, or a limitation. Those still have to go somewhere, here below the chart. Anyone who shares the chart image without the surrounding text shares the number without its caveats. That is a risk of this format.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**: 75,442 posts (35.3% of 213,491) show zero recorded views despite recorded likes, independently reproducible from `podawaa2024.json`.

**A format experiment**: this piece tests whether a chart-first structure communicates this specific finding more effectively than a text-first one. It doesn't add a new finding beyond what's already published.
