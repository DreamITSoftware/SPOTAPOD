# A Career Coach's Response to the Resume-Rewrite Template Epidemic

### Verified: 533 posts running the exact same resume-rewrite formula. What that means for coaches whose clients keep asking about it.

Career coaches regularly hear this question: "I saw this LinkedIn post with a resume trick that got 10,000 likes. Should I be doing that?" This project's data gives a concrete reason to be skeptical.

## The finding behind the question

533 posts in this project's dataset use an identical resume-rewrite template, reproduced verbatim across hundreds of separate accounts. That is not 533 professionals independently finding the same technique. It is one piece of content propagating through a network, possibly with engagement-pod amplification behind at least some of its reach, given the broader patterns this project documents in the same datasets.

## A reframe to offer clients

The question is not "does this formula work," because 533 identical copies are not evidence of effectiveness either way. Template propagation and effectiveness are separate questions, and this data can't answer both. A resume format's popularity on LinkedIn is not evidence that it works in a hiring decision, and clients chasing the most-liked version of a template are optimizing for the wrong signal. What works for a hiring manager reading a resume is a different question from what generates LinkedIn engagement.

## Using this in coaching practice

Clients often arrive anxious about a viral format they feel they are "supposed" to use. A concrete, verified number, 533 identical copies of one template, gives a coach a specific reason to redirect that anxiety toward substance instead of format-chasing, where a vague "don't just copy what you see online" carries less weight.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**: 533 identical resume-rewrite-template posts, independently reproducible.

**INFERENCE**: that engagement-pod amplification specifically drove any given instance's reach. This project does not claim to know the reach mechanism for any specific post.
