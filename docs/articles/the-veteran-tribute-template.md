# The Veteran-Tribute Template

### One political campaign post inflated a veteran-content count by 25%. The remaining content was genuine

Checking LinkedIn for veteran and military-related language turned up 476 raw matches in one dataset. A fifth of them traced back to a single source unrelated to military tribute.

## The artifact inside the number

97 of those 476 matches shared the same repeated post: a political campaign-endorsement piece describing its candidate as a "decorated 20-year veteran and combat aviator." This is the same template already documented in this project's political-content scan. It appeared here because the word "veteran" is in its text. Corrected for that one repeated post, the any-mention count is **380**, not 476.

## What remained after the correction

Unlike most duplicate-template corrections in this project, the remainder was distinct tribute content: a fighter pilot's widow continuing his legacy, a soldier's wife honoring her husband, a young army doctor killed saving fellow soldiers, a third-generation officer's family story. This project checked these individually instead of assuming they were more templated content once the obvious artifact was removed.

## Why the correction matters

A project built on catching inflated numbers has to catch its own. Finding a 25% inflation in its own veteran-content count, then verifying that the remainder is genuine, applies the same standard this project uses everywhere else.
