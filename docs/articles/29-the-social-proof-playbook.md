# The Social-Proof Playbook

### The exact mechanics this entire project studies, cataloged in one scan: awards, follower milestones, and engagement bait

Most of this project's content scans check for topics: crypto, empathy, book promotion. One scan checks for something more direct: the actual mechanics of manufactured credibility itself. Award claims, follower-count milestones, engagement-bait question formats, and congratulations or anniversary posts, counted across all three datasets.

| Category | podawaa2024 | HyperClapper | LinkBoost-2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engagement bait (Agree?/Thoughts?/etc.) | 5,072 | 173 | 1,408 |
| Congratulations / anniversary | 604 | 154 | 95 |

| Follower milestone | 93 | 47 | 184 |

## The "Agree?" template, confirmed a second time

The engagement-bait category is built directly around the already-documented "Agree?" post, 3,918 occurrences in the largest dataset, a finding significant enough to earn its own separate article in this project. This scan independently confirms the same phenomenon shows up in a second, structurally different dataset too: 888 occurrences of the same one-word engagement-bait post in LinkBoost-2025, found from a completely different angle than the original discovery.

Because both podawaa2024 and LinkBoost-2025 trigger the duplicate-template safeguard for exactly this reason, the raw "any mention" totals (6,061 and 1,712 respectively) overstate distinct content. Corrected for the "Agree?" template being counted once rather than thousands of times, the real ranges are 2,144 to 3,918 and 825 to 1,712.

## Why this scan is different from the other 40-odd topic checks in this project

Every other niche-topic scan in this repo checks what people talk about. This one checks how they get you to react: the credibility-signaling mechanics themselves, independent of subject matter. Finding the same "Agree?" pattern turn up again here, from a completely different search, is the kind of cross-confirmation that makes a finding solid rather than a one-off artifact of one particular keyword list.
