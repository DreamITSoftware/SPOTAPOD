# The Social-Proof Playbook

### The mechanics this project studies, cataloged in one scan: awards, follower milestones, and engagement bait

Most of this project's content scans check for topics: crypto, empathy, book promotion. One scan checks the mechanics of manufactured credibility: award claims, follower-count milestones, engagement-bait question formats, and congratulations or anniversary posts, counted across all three datasets.

## The "Agree?" template, confirmed a second time

The engagement-bait category is built around the already-documented "Agree?" post, with 3,918 occurrences in the largest dataset, which has its own article in this project. This scan confirms the same pattern in a second, structurally different dataset: 888 occurrences of the same one-word engagement-bait post in LinkBoost-2025, found through a different search than the original discovery.

Because both podawaa2024 and LinkBoost-2025 trigger the duplicate-template safeguard for this reason, the raw "any mention" totals (6,061 and 1,712 respectively) overstate distinct content. Corrected for the "Agree?" template being counted once rather than thousands of times, the real ranges are 2,144 to 3,918 and 825 to 1,712.

## Why this scan is different from the other 40-odd topic checks in this project

Every other niche-topic scan in this repo checks what people talk about. This one checks how posts get readers to react, independent of subject matter. Finding the same "Agree?" pattern again from a different search makes it less likely to be an artifact of one keyword list.
