# AI Mentions Up, Blockchain Down: A Two-Year Snapshot

### Technology and AI content grew consistently across both later datasets. Blockchain and crypto content shrank, and this is confirmed two ways.

Unlike the career-content jump examined elsewhere in this series, the AI-up, blockchain-down pattern shows up across independent sources, which makes it a more trustworthy trend claim.

## The AI number

`technology_ai` content share was 16.39% in the earlier-skewing podawaa2024 dataset, rising to 27.97% and 25.29% in HyperClapper and LinkBoost-2025, both later-dated datasets showing a similar increase, roughly 9 to 12 percentage points higher than the earlier baseline. Unlike the career-content comparison in a companion piece, this pattern replicates across both independent later sources, which is a stronger basis for treating it as a real trend rather than a single dataset's quirk.

## The blockchain number

This project's separate research into blockchain and crypto content documented its declining share over the same window that AI mentions were climbing. The two trends moved in opposite directions, consistent with an attention shift in tech-adjacent LinkedIn content from blockchain toward AI.

## Why consistency across datasets matters

The main methodological lesson from this project's comparative work is that a pattern confirmed in only one dataset deserves more hedging than a pattern confirmed in two independent ones. The AI-content increase meets that bar. Several other candidate "trends" checked elsewhere in this series do not. This distinguishes a claim this project calls CORROBORATED from one that stays at UNCORROBORATED or INFERENCE.

## What this doesn't establish

Two datasets agreeing is stronger than one, but it is not a controlled longitudinal study of the same population over time. These are three separately sourced tools with different user bases. The trend is real within what is measurable here. Three engagement-pod exports cannot show whether it generalizes to LinkedIn content as a whole.

## What this is, and isn't

**CORROBORATED**: technology and AI content share increasing from the earlier dataset to both later ones (16.39% to 27.97% and 25.29%).

**VERIFIED**, independently, in this project's blockchain-content research: a declining share of crypto and blockchain mentions over the comparable window.
