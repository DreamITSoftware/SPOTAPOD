# The Anniversary Post: One Year of SPOTAPOD, What Changed

### A template for the retrospective this project will write once a year has passed

This project doesn't yet have a full year of history behind it. This piece is the prepared format of the retrospective it will publish once it does, so the future version is written to a standard decided now rather than improvised around an anniversary date.

## What the retrospective will need to cover

**Every correction made, as well as every finding published.** The corrections log (1,706 to 533, 50.6%/87.3% to 36.2%/83.5%, "nine of ten" to "four of ten") is as much a record of this project's first year as any new finding. A retrospective that lists only new discoveries and omits corrected errors would be the selective accounting this project's evidence-tier discipline exists to prevent.

**Which datasets were added, and how each was actually vetted.** The spotapod.json investigation, confirming it was a derived aggregation of podawaa2024.json rather than a genuine fourth independent dataset, is an example of a due-diligence step that belongs in a retrospective, because getting it wrong (claiming a fourth independent dataset that was not one) would have been an avoidable error.

**What changed in methodology, and why.** Any evolution in how evidence tiers are applied, how keyword-based categories are checked for collisions (the "cat" false positive belongs here), or how privacy architecture was hardened over the year.

## Why write the template early

Deciding the standard in advance, that corrections and missteps get equal billing with new findings, removes the temptation to write a flattering highlight reel when an anniversary arrives.

## What this is, and isn't

**A prepared format, not a completed retrospective.** This project has not yet reached a full year of published work as of this writing.
