# The Anniversary Post: One Year of SPOTAPOD, What Changed

### A template for the retrospective this project will actually be able to write once a year has genuinely passed

This project doesn't yet have a full year of history behind it. This piece is the prepared shape of the retrospective it will publish once it does, so that future version is written to a standard decided now, not improvised under the pull of a nice round anniversary date.

## What the actual retrospective will need to account for

**Every correction made, not just every finding published.** The corrections log (1,706 to 533, 50.6%/87.3% to 36.2%/83.5%, "nine of ten" to "four of ten") is as much a measure of this project's first year as any new finding. A retrospective that only lists new discoveries while omitting its own corrected errors would be exactly the kind of selective accounting this project's evidence-tier discipline exists to prevent.

**Which datasets were added, and how each was actually vetted.** The spotapod.json investigation, confirming it was a derived aggregation of podawaa2024.json rather than a genuine fourth independent dataset, is a good example of the kind of due-diligence step that belongs in a retrospective, precisely because getting it wrong (claiming a fourth independent dataset that wasn't one) would have been a real, avoidable error.

**What changed in methodology, and why.** Any evolution in how evidence tiers are applied, how keyword-based categories are checked for collisions (the "cat" false positive belongs here), or how privacy architecture was hardened over the year.

## Why write the template before the year is up

Deciding the retrospective's honesty standard in advance, specifically, that corrections and missteps get equal billing with new findings, removes the temptation to write a flattering highlight reel once there's an actual anniversary date and actual momentum to protect.

## What this is, and isn't

**A prepared format, not a completed retrospective.** This project has not yet reached a full year of published work as of this writing.
