# One Post: Series Launch

### A recurring deep-dive format on a single anomalous record per installment, without ever showing the record itself

The obvious version of a "one post" deep-dive series would display an actual post and dissect it. This project can't do that without risking re-identification, so this series does something narrower and, on reflection, more honest. One installment, one specific anomaly type, described entirely through its measurable properties, never its actual content.

## Installment one: the 88,028-likes-zero-views post

**What's measurable about it:** a single record in `podawaa2024.json` with a like count of 88,028 and a view count of 0. That specific combination, real likes, zero views, is the largest single instance of this project's zero-view anomaly pattern in the entire dataset.

**What that combination would require, if the metrics were genuine:** 88,028 individual people encountering and liking a post that was, per the same record, viewed by nobody. That's not a small discrepancy. It's a metric relationship that has no plausible innocent explanation under normal platform mechanics.

**What this project doesn't know, and won't claim to know:** which account posted it, when precisely, what it said, or why this specific record shows this specific anomaly rather than a smaller one. The record is one data point in an aggregate pattern, examined in isolation, not a case study of an identified individual's behavior.

## Why this format works within this project's constraints

Depth doesn't require identity. This installment goes deeper into a single record's numbers than any of this project's aggregate-statistics pieces do, while disclosing nothing that could trace back to a real account. It's proof that "one post, examined closely" and "one person, exposed" are genuinely different projects, and this series only does the first.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**: this specific like/view combination exists in the dataset, independently reproducible.

**Anonymized by design, not by omission**: no identifying detail about this record is available to reveal, because none was ever retained.
