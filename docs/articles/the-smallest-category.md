# The Smallest Category: Series Launch

### A recurring feature on the rarest topic in this project's data, starting with seven mentions across 340,829 posts

Most of this project's writing is about common patterns, the ones big enough to be a problem. This series covers the opposite. Each installment reports the smallest category in the taxonomy.

## Installment one: rare-earth metals

Across 340,829 posts spanning this project's topic taxonomy, mentions of rare-earth metals, a subject with current relevance to technology supply chains and geopolitics, appeared exactly **seven** times. Seven posts, out of about a third of a million.

## Why the smallest category gets a series

A single rare-topic finding is a trivia fact. A recurring series of them shows what a professional-networking platform's content mix looks like at its margins: which real-world topics get almost no coverage regardless of their importance, and which niche topics do show up. This complements the project's usual focus on the largest patterns.

## What counts as an installment

Any category from this project's taxonomy work that falls below roughly 0.01% of a dataset's total posts, reported with its exact count and the dataset it came from, with no commentary on why it is rare beyond what the data supports.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**: seven rare-earth-metals mentions across 340,829 posts, independently reproducible from this project's topic taxonomy.

**No causal claim**: this project doesn't attempt to explain why a given topic is rare, only to report that it is.
