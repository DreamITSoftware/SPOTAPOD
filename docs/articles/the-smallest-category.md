# The Smallest Category: Series Launch

### A recurring feature on whatever topic turned out to be rarest in this project's data, starting with seven mentions across 340,829 posts

Most of this project's writing is about what's common, the patterns big enough to be a problem. This series flips that. Each installment finds and reports the smallest, rarest category in the taxonomy, just because it's an interesting shape to notice.

## Installment one: rare-earth metals

Across 340,829 posts spanning this project's topic taxonomy, mentions of rare-earth metals, a subject with real, current relevance to technology supply chains and geopolitics, appeared exactly **seven** times. Not seven percent. Seven posts, total, out of a third of a million.

## Why the smallest category is worth a series of its own

A single rare-topic finding is a trivia fact. A recurring series of them starts to sketch the actual shape of what a professional-networking platform's content mix looks like at its margins, which real-world topics get essentially no oxygen at all, regardless of their objective importance, versus which niche topics do show up. That's a different and complementary lens to this project's usual focus on the largest, most common patterns.

## What counts as an installment

Any category from this project's ongoing taxonomy work that falls below roughly 0.01% of a dataset's total posts, reported with its exact count and the dataset it came from, no editorializing about why it's rare beyond what the data can actually support.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**: seven rare-earth-metals mentions across 340,829 posts, independently reproducible from this project's topic taxonomy.

**No causal claim**: this project doesn't attempt to explain why a given topic is rare, only to report that it is.
