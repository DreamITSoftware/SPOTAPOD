# The Book-Launch Formula

### Under 1% of LinkedIn is book promotion, and the genre breakdown shows what LinkedIn authors sell

Book-promotion language ("my new book," "pre-order," "bestseller," "grab your copy") is a small minority everywhere it was checked: 0.26% of a 213,491-post dataset, 0.97% of a 49,369-post dataset, 0.98% of a 77,969-record dataset. It defines none of the three.

## What that small slice is about

By genre, business and self-help dominate and fiction barely registers.

- **Business/entrepreneurship** leads in all three datasets (32.5% to 49.2% of book-promotion records).
- **Writing craft** is second almost everywhere, peaking at 51.0% in HyperClapper.
- **Self-help/personal development** is large in HyperClapper (50.2%) but does not appear as a separate category in LinkBoost-2025.
- **Fiction/novel** is rare: 1.6% in podawaa2024, 1.3% in HyperClapper, and even in LinkBoost-2025, where it's highest, just 7.4%.
- **Poetry** appears only in podawaa2024, at 0.9%.

## Reading the breakdown

LinkedIn's book-promotion content looks like career advice more than a books community: self-published business and self-help authors promoting their books for career credibility, not writers sharing creative work. Fiction and poetry are nearly absent, and business and writing-craft framing dominates, which suggests the kind of "author" LinkedIn hosts.
