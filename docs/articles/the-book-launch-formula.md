# The Book-Launch Formula

### Under 1% of LinkedIn is book promotion, but the genre breakdown reveals what LinkedIn authors are actually selling

Book-promotion language ("my new book," "pre-order," "bestseller," "grab your copy") is a small minority everywhere it was checked: 0.26% of a 213,491-post dataset, 0.97% of a 49,369-post dataset, 0.98% of a 77,969-record dataset. Nowhere close to a defining feature of any of the three.

## What that small slice is actually about

Break the matching records down by genre and a pattern shows up immediately. Business and self-help dominate. Fiction barely registers.

- **Business/entrepreneurship** leads in all three datasets (32.5% to 49.2% of book-promotion records).
- **Writing craft** is second almost everywhere, peaking at 51.0% in HyperClapper.
- **Self-help/personal development** is huge in HyperClapper specifically (50.2%) but doesn't even register as a separate category in LinkBoost-2025.
- **Fiction/novel** barely shows up: 1.6% in podawaa2024, 1.3% in HyperClapper, and even in LinkBoost-2025, where it's highest, just 7.4%.
- **Poetry** appears only in podawaa2024, at 0.9%.

## Reading the shape of it

LinkedIn's book-promotion content isn't really a books platform in miniature. It's a career-advice platform that happens to include self-published business and self-help authors promoting their own books as a career-credibility move, not writers sharing creative work. The near-absence of fiction and poetry, against the dominance of business and writing-craft framing, tells you what kind of "author" LinkedIn actually hosts.
