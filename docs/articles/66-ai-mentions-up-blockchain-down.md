# AI Mentions Up, Blockchain Down: A Two-Year Snapshot

### Technology and AI content grew consistently across both later datasets. Blockchain and crypto content shrank, and this one's confirmed two ways, not just one.

Unlike the career-content jump examined elsewhere in this series, the AI-up, blockchain-down pattern shows up consistently across independent sources, which is exactly what makes it a more trustworthy trend claim.

## The AI number

`technology_ai` content share was 16.39% in the earlier-skewing podawaa2024 dataset, rising to 27.97% and 25.29% in HyperClapper and LinkBoost-2025, both later-dated datasets showing a broadly similar increase, roughly 9 to 12 percentage points higher than the earlier baseline. Unlike the career-content comparison in a companion piece, this pattern replicates across both independent later sources, which is a meaningfully stronger basis for treating it as a real trend rather than a single dataset's quirk.

## The blockchain number

This project's separate research into blockchain and crypto content documented its declining share over the same window that AI mentions were climbing, the two trends moving in opposite directions within the same content ecosystem, consistent with a broader attention shift in tech-adjacent LinkedIn content away from one hype cycle and toward another.

## Why consistency across datasets matters this much

The single biggest methodological lesson from this project's comparative work is that a pattern confirmed in only one dataset deserves much more hedging than a pattern confirmed in two independent ones. The AI-content increase clears that bar. Several other candidate "trends" checked elsewhere in this series don't. That's not a minor technical footnote. It's the difference between a claim this project is willing to call CORROBORATED and one that stays at UNCORROBORATED or INFERENCE.

## What this doesn't establish

Two datasets agreeing is stronger than one, but it's still not a controlled longitudinal study of the same population over time. These remain three separately sourced tools with different user bases. The trend is real within what's measurable here. Whether it generalizes to LinkedIn content as a whole is a claim beyond what three engagement-pod exports can support.

## What this is, and isn't

**CORROBORATED**: technology and AI content share increasing from the earlier dataset to both later ones (16.39% to 27.97% and 25.29%).

**VERIFIED**, independently, in this project's blockchain-content research: a declining share of crypto and blockchain mentions over the comparable window.
