# Tesla Owns LinkedIn

### Every carmaker mentioned across three LinkedIn datasets, and Tesla beats the runner-up by more than 2 to 1

Count every mention of a major automaker's name across three LinkedIn datasets and one brand leads the rest. **Tesla** has 410 mentions in a 213,491-post dataset, 63 in a 49,369-post dataset, and 411 in a 77,969-record dataset. In two of the three files, that is more than double the next-closest competitor.

| Maker | podawaa2024 | HyperClapper | LinkBoost-2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tesla | 410 | 63 | 411 |
| Ford | 159 | 23 | 66 |
| Mercedes(-Benz) | 130 | 22 | 44 |
| Audi | 87 | 5 | - |
| BMW | 81 | 16 | - |
| Toyota | 75 | 17 | 19 |

## In one dataset, Tesla isn't a car story at all

In LinkBoost-2025, Tesla's 411 mentions were checked directly against the actual post text, and the mentions were financial, not automotive. The distinct posts behind those mentions include a JPMorgan analyst note on humanoid-robot stocks tied to Tesla, "should you buy a Tesla in cash" investment advice, general Musk and AI business commentary, and a Cathie Wood interview excerpt. In this dataset, Tesla on LinkedIn is a stock-and-founder topic.

## Why the posts were checked before writing the headline

A simpler version of this piece would report "Tesla dominates automotive discussion on LinkedIn" and stop. That would hide a real distinction. podawaa2024 and HyperClapper's Tesla mentions plausibly include genuine car-buyer and industry discussion. LinkBoost-2025's do not seem to, once read. The word and count method are the same, but the content differs.
