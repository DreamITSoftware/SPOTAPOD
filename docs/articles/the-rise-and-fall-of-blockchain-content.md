# The Rise and Fall of Blockchain Content

### Crypto talk is nearly 3% of LinkedIn's older dataset and under 1% of its newer one

Blockchain and crypto-related terms (blockchain, crypto, Web3, NFT, Bitcoin, DeFi, Ethereum) appear in **2.930%** of a 213,491-post 2024 dataset, but only **0.774%** of a 49,369-post dataset drawn from 2025 into 2026. That's nearly a 4x drop in prevalence.

## The breakdown behind the drop

| Term | podawaa2024 | HyperClapper |
|---|---|---|
| Blockchain | 2,971 | 113 |
| Crypto/cryptocurrency | 2,361 | 196 |
| Web3 | 2,251 | 65 |
| NFT | 1,541 | 19 |
| Bitcoin | 1,112 | 87 |
| DeFi | 551 | 29 |
| Ethereum | 515 | 29 |

Every single term drops, not just the average. Web3 mentions fall from 2,251 to 65, NFT mentions from 1,541 to 19. This isn't one outlier term skewing the total. It's a broad retreat across the entire crypto vocabulary.

## A plausible story, clearly labeled as one

It's tempting to read this as "AI replaced blockchain as LinkedIn's hype cycle of choice," and this project's own separate scans do show AI-related mentions climbing sharply across the same rough time window. But this specific comparison only establishes the blockchain decline itself, not a causal link to AI's rise. The two trends are documented separately in this project. Connecting them as cause and effect would be going beyond what either scan actually measures.

## What this is, and isn't

**VERIFIED**, reproducible via the published scan: the prevalence rates and term breakdown above, computed identically across both files.

**INFERENCE**: that AI content specifically displaced blockchain content. The two trends are both real and both documented, but a causal connection between them is not established by this comparison alone.
